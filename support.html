Are the prices live?

Fuel prices in Bahrain are set once a month by the Fuel Price Committee, and they usually start on the 2nd. Banzeen checks for new prices by itself and updates within a few hours of each announcement. If the prices are more than a month old, the app shows a warning. Always confirm the price at the pump.

How do I add my car?

Open My Cars and tap the plus button. Choose the brand, then the car, then the engine. The tank size, fuel use and fuel type fill in for you. If your car is not listed, choose "My car is not listed" and type the details yourself.

How do I delete or change a car?

Tap the car in My Cars to change it. To delete it, open the car and tap Delete Car at the bottom, or press and hold the car in the list.

How do I choose my main car?

The main car is the one shown first on Home and in the My Car widget. Swipe between your cars on Home, or press and hold a car in My Cars and tap Make main car.

The fuel use looks different from my car

The numbers in the app are typical values. Real fuel use depends on traffic, speed and air conditioning. Open the car, then Details, and type the number from your trip computer.

How do I add the widgets?

Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen or lock screen, tap the plus or Customize button, search for Banzeen, and choose Fuel Prices or My Car.

How do I change the language or the look?

Open Settings in the app. You can choose English or Arabic, and Automatic, Light or Dark.

Does Banzeen collect my data?

No. Read the Privacy Policy.
