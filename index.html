Banzeen

Know what your fuel will cost before you drive.

Banzeen is an iPhone app for drivers in Bahrain. It shows the official monthly fuel prices, saves your car, and tells you what a trip or a full tank will cost. It works in English and Arabic, in light and dark mode, and without an account.

Open price data

The prices Banzeen uses are free for anyone at v1/latest.json. They are checked against two news sites before they are published.
